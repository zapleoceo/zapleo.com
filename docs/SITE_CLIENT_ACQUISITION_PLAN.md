# Zapleo: client-acquisition plan

## Positioning

Zapleo is the personal business site for Dmitriy Zaporozhets, an operator who designs and ships AI-assisted operational systems. The primary buyer is an owner or sales leader already paying for inbound leads and suspecting that response, handoff or attribution failures are wasting spend. The site does not present Dmitriy as a current IT STEP director or as a traditional software engineer.

## Visitor path

1. The homepage names a concrete pain: a paid-for lead disappears between advertising, CRM, calls, messages and ownership.
2. A dedicated `/revenue-recovery/` page explains the ten-business-day diagnosis: one funnel, one CRM, one primary communication channel, up to 30 days of history.
3. Past-work proof is limited to verifiable scope and process: 1,956 calls audited; 10,879 messages reviewed over 60 days; ad-to-lead attribution coverage changed from 45% to 93.6%. None of these figures is presented as a client sales result.
4. The visitor can send a prefilled email describing monthly lead volume, CRM, communication channel and suspected gap. Contact details and selected work remain one click away.

## Offer and economics

- Fixed project fee: $5,000, with $2,500 to start and $2,500 after agreed deliverables are reviewed.
- Deliverables: loss map with data-quality caveats, opportunity model with explicit assumptions, and a first-fix blueprint for one pilot.
- Production integration, ongoing management and guaranteed revenue are excluded. A separate implementation engagement can be proposed only after the diagnosis.
- Two clients produce $10,000 in **received payments** within 30 days only if both projects start, finish and both final payments arrive within that period. Two deposits alone produce $5,000. This is a commercial hypothesis, not a forecast.
- Capacity: at 10–15 hours a week, do not sell more than two overlapping sprints without renegotiating scope or capacity.

## Distribution and validation

The website is a conversion destination, not a traffic source. Start with warm business contacts and precise outreach to lead-heavy education, service or hospitality businesses in markets where Dmitriy can work remotely. Link directly to the service page; do not send generic traffic to a portfolio and hope it converts. Track outreach, replies, qualified calls, written proposals, deposits, completed reviews and final payments. Keep personally identifiable lead data out of public case studies.

Dmitriy confirmed that `dima@zapleo.com` receives mail. Before scaling outreach, send one test inquiry from outside the domain, reply to it, and check the mobile CTA. The current static site deliberately uses email rather than a third-party form or tracker. If buyers fail to use email, add a consent-aware scheduling/form flow after measuring that friction; do not silently collect lead data through an unverified provider.

## Review after launch

- Check analytics only if a privacy-safe tool is configured; otherwise measure tagged outreach links and actual inquiries manually.
- After 20 targeted visits, inspect whether people reach the service page and whether they contact Dmitriy. The sample is too small for a conversion-rate claim, but it can reveal broken links or misunderstood positioning.
- After 10 qualified conversations, classify objections to price, access, scope and proof. Rewrite copy based on those objections, not on invented success stories.
- Revisit price, payment timing and scope if the day-10 call/proposal gate or day-18 deposit gate from the wider 30-day plan is missed.
